# Nonogram v49 QA Report

Release: v49
Date: 2026-11-16

## Change
- Fill and Cross drag gestures now lock to one axis per pointer stroke.
- The dominant initial movement selects a single row (horizontal) or column (vertical).
- Diagonal drift is projected onto the locked row/column and cannot paint adjacent lines.
- A short 8px movement dead zone avoids accidental axis selection from touch jitter.
- Tap behaviour remains single-cell; the existing fixed-intent fill/cross/erase model is unchanged.
- Service-worker cache bumped to v13 to invalidate stale input code.
