About Nonogram

Nonogram is a picture-logic puzzle. Read the numbers beside each row and above each column, then fill the squares that belong. Each number tells you the length of a consecutive run.

Complete every row and column to uncover the hidden pattern. No timer, no rush. Just a little logic, one square at a time.
