INPUT FIX
---------
A stroke locks its target from the first tile. Fill on an empty tile fills the
whole path; Fill starting on a filled tile erases the path. Cross behaves the
same way for crossed tiles. Each visited cell is processed once. The target
never flips midway through the same gesture.
