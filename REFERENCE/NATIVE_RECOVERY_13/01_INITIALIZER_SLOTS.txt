Easy's effective slot correction
--------------------------------
0x114fffC: ADRP X21, 0x1aa9000
0x1150008: B 0x115fc6c
0x115fc6c: LDR X21, [X21, #0x7a8]
0x115fc70: B 0x115000c

Thus X21 is loaded from slot 0x1aa97a8, not from the 0x1aa9000 page base.
The relocation at 0x1aa97a8 points to 0x1b29170.

The other effective slots and relocation targets are listed in
CORE/06_LEVELSIZES_HANDLES.md. The first word at each target is a metadata usage
token, not an Int32 dimension and not a runtime pointer before initialization.
